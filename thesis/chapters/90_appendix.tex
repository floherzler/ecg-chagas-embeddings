\paragraph{Example 1.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Goal}: Identify additional open-source deep-learning strategies for 1D ECG classification and augmentation beyond the current ResNet-18 setup.
  \item \textbf{Prompt (excerpt)}: \emph{``Since you are able to search the web, can you suggest some open-source papers for other DL strategies for this 1D ECG data for me to look at?''}
  \item \textbf{AI output (excerpt)}: A curated list of ECG SSL/representation-learning and time-series baselines with links to papers/code repositories (including augmentation-focused work such as Qiu et al., EMBC 2022).
  \item \textbf{Impact on process/product}: Used to extend the Discussion (architecture generalization and augmentation limitations) and to add targeted literature citations in Background/Discussion.
  \item \textbf{Your revision}: Recommendations were filtered for direct relevance to ECG settings and then integrated only where consistent with reported results and thesis scope.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Example 2.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Goal}: Improve thesis readability and page flow by fixing table/figure placement, heading density in TOC, and oversized composite graphics in Results/Appendix.
  \item \textbf{Prompt (excerpt)}: \emph{``Please include the referenced tables closer to their point of reference ... move the results tables closer to their place in the text in general.''} and \emph{``The ST-DFT-LRP sample figures are taking up a lot of space ... make them smaller so that 3 fit on the same page and stack them using LaTeX.''}
  \item \textbf{AI output (excerpt)}: Suggested and applied targeted LaTeX edits (float placement options, grouped figure layouts, caption consolidation, and heading-level demotion from subsection to paragraph* where appropriate).
  \item \textbf{Impact on process/product}: Improved visual consistency, reduced TOC clutter, better proximity of figures/tables to corresponding interpretation paragraphs, and clearer presentation of stage-wise results.
  \item \textbf{Your revision}: Final placement and style decisions were manually checked in the compiled PDF and adjusted iteratively based on domain-specific narrative priorities.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Example 3.}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{Goal}: Verify that methodological descriptions and formulas in the thesis match the actual implementation in scripts and notebooks.
  \item \textbf{Prompt (excerpt)}: \emph{``Please check if all components of the used formulas in background are explained ...''} and \emph{``Please do the same for methods ... check if they could be better explained.''}
  \item \textbf{AI output (excerpt)}: Performed code-text consistency checks, identified ambiguous or undefined symbols in formulas, and proposed concrete wording patches aligned with implementation details (e.g., top-$k$ definitions, indexing, stability constants).
  \item \textbf{Impact on process/product}: Reduced risk of method-reporting mismatch, improved reproducibility of the written methodology, and increased clarity for reviewers.
  \item \textbf{Your revision}: Final decisions on formula scope and level of mathematical detail were retained by the author to match thesis audience and chapter goals.
\end{itemize}
